Class Features, Species & Feats

Extra Attack

Two attacks with the Attack action. Replace one attack to command one Beast's Strike.

Archery / Sharpshooter

Archery +2 is included in bow attacks. Longbows 150/600 ft.; ignore Half/Three-Quarters Cover and ranged-weapon Disadvantage from long range or enemies within 5 ft.

Deft Explorer / Roving

Perception Expertise; Climb and Swim 40 ft. No Heavy armor.

Primal Companion / Exceptional Training

One companion. Your Bonus Action commands its Action and permits its own Bonus Action Dash/Disengage/Dodge/Help. Attack replacement alone does not grant that bonus command. Its attack damage can be Force.

Inspiring Leader

After a Short or Long Rest and performance: up to six witnessing allies within 30 ft., including you, gain 12 Temp HP. No stacking or automatic use.

Chthonic Legacy / Magic Initiate

WIS casting. Four cantrips and four always-prepared leveled grants are separate from Ranger selections.

Darkvision / Defenses

Darkvision 60 ft.; Necrotic resistance and Poison resistance. Swim Speed equals walking Speed; Advantage on saves to avoid grapples and checks to escape.

Energy Bow options

Arrows shed 20 ft. Bright + 20 ft. Dim Light and vanish on hit/miss. Restraint instead of damage: ranged hit against a creature, DC 15 STR or Restrained 1 minute; action/DC 20 STR (Athletics) to escape. Magic action transport: visible willing Medium-or-smaller creature or unattended object fitting a 5-ft. cube within 60 ft. to a visible unoccupied space within 10 ft. Magic action ladder: wall within 60 ft.; ladder up to 60 ft., lasts 1 minute.
