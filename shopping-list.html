Emergency Supplies

Revival

  • Revivify Scroll

    Price: 4,500 GP

    Revives a creature within 1 minute of death with 1 HP, using an Action and touch. Cannot reverse old-age death or restore missing body parts. No separate diamond is needed for scroll use. Requires an eligible reader; casting above their normal spell level requires a DC 13 spellcasting-ability check, with the scroll lost on failure.

    Revivify spell reference on D&D Beyond

Potion Options

Healing Potions

Our campaign rule: drink as a Bonus Action for rolled healing, or spend an Action to drink for maximum healing. Maximum healing when administering a potion to someone else is not established by this exception.

  • Potion of Healing

    Price: 50 GP

    Restores 2d4+2 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 10 HP.

    Healing potion family on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Greater Healing

    Price: 100 GP

    Restores 4d4+4 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 20 HP.

    Greater Healing on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Superior Healing

    Price: 200 GP

    Restores 8d4+8 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 40 HP.

    Healing potion family on D&D Beyond (includes Superior)

  • Potion of Supreme Healing

    Price: 400 GP

    Restores 10d4+20 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 60 HP.

    Supreme Healing on D&D Beyond

Prevention and Recovery

Detox Options

  • Antitoxin

    Price: 50 GP

    Drink as a Bonus Action for Advantage on saves to avoid or end Poisoned for 1 hour. Helps resist poison; does not automatically cure it or grant Poison damage resistance.

    Antitoxin on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Vitality

    Price: 900 GP

    Removes all Exhaustion and ends Poisoned. Also maximizes healing from spent Hit Dice for 24 hours. The 2024 version is not a general disease cure.

    Potion of Vitality on D&D Beyond

Utility and Support

Other Supplies and Equipment

  • Bracers of Archery

    Price: 200 GP

    Grants proficiency with Longbows and Shortbows and +2 to damage rolls with those weapons while worn. Does not add to attack rolls or crossbow damage. Requires attunement.

    Bracers of Archery on D&D Beyond

  • Ring of Poison Resistance

    Price: 80 GP

    Grants Resistance to Poison damage while worn, halving that damage. Does not grant immunity to the Poisoned condition or cure poison. The 2024 Ring of Resistance does not require attunement.

    Ring of Resistance on D&D Beyond (Poison variant)

  • Black Water

    Price: 25 GP

    Industrial runoff purified into cheap medicine. Water used to cool magic-powered machines collects their excess magical energy.

    Campaign item; no fixed healing amount or guaranteed mechanical effect is specified here. No verified D&D Beyond reference.

  • Caltrops

    Price: 5 SP per pound

    Spend a Utilize action to cover a 5-foot square within 5 feet. A creature entering for the first time on a turn makes a DC 15 Dexterity save or takes 1 Piercing damage and has Speed 0 until its next turn starts. Affects allies too; recovering the caltrops takes 10 minutes.

    Caltrops on D&D Beyond

  • Slippers of Spider Climbing

    Price: 500 GP

    Walk on walls and ceilings with hands free; Climb Speed equals walking Speed. Does not work on slippery surfaces such as oil or ice. Requires attunement.

    Slippers of Spider Climbing on D&D Beyond

  • Amulet of Health

    Price: 8,000 GP

    Sets Constitution to 19 while worn, improving HP and Constitution saves if that raises your modifier. No effect if Constitution is already 19 or higher. Requires attunement.

    Amulet of Health on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Heroism

    Price: 180 GP

    For 1 hour, grants 10 temporary HP and Bless (+1d4 to attacks and saves) without Concentration. Does not stack with another Bless or add together with other temporary HP.

    Potion of Heroism on D&D Beyond

  • Pearl of Power

    Price: 6,000 GP

    Use a Magic action to recover one spent spell slot of level 3 or lower, once per dawn. Does not create access to higher-level slots. Requires attunement by a spellcaster.

    Pearl of Power on D&D Beyond

  • Bag of Beans

    Price: 6,000 GP

    Plant a bean in dirt or sand and water it for a DM-chosen or random magical effect 1 minute later. Results can be helpful, strange, or dangerous. Dumping beans instead causes a 10-foot-radius explosion: DC 15 Dexterity save for half of 5d4 Force damage, affecting allies and the user too. Dumped beans are consumed; confirm how many remain before buying. No attunement.

    Bag of Beans on D&D Beyond

  • Alchemy Jug

    Price: 2,000 GP

    Use a Magic action to select a listed liquid, then a Utilize action to pour it, up to 2 gallons per minute. Options include fresh water, oil, acid, basic poison, and mayonnaise, each with its own quantity limit. Cannot switch liquids or replenish an exhausted allowance until the next dawn. No attunement.

    Alchemy Jug on D&D Beyond (may require book access)

  • Portable Hole

    Price: 8,000 GP

    A folding cloth that opens into a 6-foot-wide, 10-foot-deep extradimensional storage space on a solid surface. Opening or closing it takes a Magic action; contents remain inside when folded. It cannot tunnel through walls. Air when closed lasts 1 hour divided among breathing occupants. No attunement.

    Keep it out of a Bag of Holding or similar extradimensional item: nesting them destroys both and opens a dangerous Astral gate.

    Portable Hole on D&D Beyond

  • Immovable Rod

    Price: 3,000 GP

    Press its button as a Utilize action to fix the rod in place, even in midair. Any creature can use a Utilize action to press the button again and release it. Supports up to 8,000 pounds; excess weight deactivates it. A Utilize action and successful DC 30 Strength (Athletics) check can move the fixed rod up to 10 feet. No attunement.

    Immovable Rod on D&D Beyond

  • Decanter of Endless Water

    Price: 1,500 GP

    Use a Magic action to produce fresh or salt water: 1 gallon, 5 gallons, or a 30-gallon geyser, flowing until your next turn starts. The geyser forms a 30-foot-long, 1-foot-wide Line; one chosen creature makes a DC 13 Strength save or takes 1d4 Bludgeoning damage and falls Prone. Alternatively, knock over an unattended object weighing up to 200 pounds. No attunement.

    Decanter of Endless Water on D&D Beyond
