Rules and references

Descriptions use the 2024 rules, with our campaign's healing-potion exception noted below. D&D Beyond links are reference pages, not shop listings. Attunement requirements shown are the standard rules; campaign exceptions remain up to the DM.

Emergency Supplies

Revival

  • Revivify Scroll

    Revives a creature within 1 minute of death with 1 HP, using an Action and touch. Cannot reverse old-age death or restore missing body parts. No separate diamond is needed for scroll use. Requires an eligible reader; casting above their normal spell level requires a DC 13 spellcasting-ability check, with the scroll lost on failure.

    Revivify spell reference on D&D Beyond

Potion Options

Healing Potions

Our campaign rule: drink as a Bonus Action for rolled healing, or spend an Action to drink for maximum healing. Maximum healing when administering a potion to someone else is not established by this exception.

  • Potion of Healing

    Restores 2d4+2 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 10 HP.

    Healing potion family on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Greater Healing

    Restores 4d4+4 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 20 HP.

    Greater Healing on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Superior Healing

    Restores 8d4+8 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 40 HP.

    Healing potion family on D&D Beyond (includes Superior)

  • Potion of Supreme Healing

    Restores 10d4+20 HP; our Action-to-drink maximum is 60 HP.

    Supreme Healing on D&D Beyond

Prevention and Recovery

Detox Options

  • Antitoxin

    Drink as a Bonus Action for Advantage on saves to avoid or end Poisoned for 1 hour. Helps resist poison; does not automatically cure it or grant Poison damage resistance.

    Antitoxin on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Vitality

    Removes all Exhaustion and ends Poisoned. Also maximizes healing from spent Hit Dice for 24 hours. The 2024 version is not a general disease cure.

    Potion of Vitality on D&D Beyond

Utility and Support

Other Supplies and Equipment

  • Caltrops

    Spend a Utilize action to cover a 5-foot square within 5 feet. A creature entering for the first time on a turn makes a DC 15 Dexterity save or takes 1 Piercing damage and has Speed 0 until its next turn starts. Affects allies too; recovering the caltrops takes 10 minutes.

    Caltrops on D&D Beyond

  • Slippers of Spider Climbing

    Walk on walls and ceilings with hands free; Climb Speed equals walking Speed. Does not work on slippery surfaces such as oil or ice. Requires attunement.

    Slippers of Spider Climbing on D&D Beyond

  • Amulet of Health

    Sets Constitution to 19 while worn, improving HP and Constitution saves if that raises your modifier. No effect if Constitution is already 19 or higher. Requires attunement.

    Amulet of Health on D&D Beyond

  • Potion of Heroism

    For 1 hour, grants 10 temporary HP and Bless (+1d4 to attacks and saves) without Concentration. Does not stack with another Bless or add together with other temporary HP.

    Potion of Heroism on D&D Beyond

  • Pearl of Power

    Use a Magic action to recover one spent spell slot of level 3 or lower, once per dawn. Does not create access to higher-level slots. Requires attunement by a spellcaster.

    Pearl of Power on D&D Beyond
