Session 1: A Ritual in Ruins

Rebel rings, gunpowder, and an interrupted flight.

Session 1 spoilers

This page reveals the complete events of the first published session.

Recap

Into the Fight Club

The party began in an unfamiliar town while searching for local rebels. Disguised with rebel rings, they entered a fight club and found Drau among a group preparing an extraordinary ritual. The rebels claimed they would end slavery by dragging down a god and compelling him to abolish every monarchy.

Rather than let the ritual continue, the party infiltrated the ceremony and destroyed it with two barrels of gunpowder. The ritual site was left in ruins, but its leader escaped.

Aftermath

A Dragon Returned

The party returned Rau, an ice dragon, to a Dragon Bank and received a reward. Their history with the dragons was complicated: an earlier clash had ended with one of the dragons killed and later revived.

The group then boarded an airship to collect another reward. Several days into the trip, a second airship intercepted them with an urgent message: the true Filaretos wanted to see them and had sent for them personally.

Where We Left Off

Bound for Andrissa

The original journey was abandoned as the party transferred to Filaretos's airship, uncertain why they had become such a high priority.
